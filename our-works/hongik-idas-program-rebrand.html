Project

Hongik IDAS Program Rebrand

Identity Design · Education

GDLA Studio

A rebrand for a graduate design-management program, translating an academic mission into a mark and system that reads clearly on diplomas, signage, and admissions materials alike.

Background

A graduate design-management program needed its identity to speak to two audiences at once: prospective students weighing a serious academic choice, and the institution's own diplomas, signage, and formal materials that outlive any single marketing cycle.

Insight

The rebrand translates the program's academic mission into a mark restrained enough to sit comfortably on a diploma, yet distinctive enough to stand out in an admissions brochure — one system doing double duty across the program's most formal and most persuasive materials.
